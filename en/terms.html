Terms of Service

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Version française

1. Purpose

These terms govern the use of Pulse Studio, a Windows desktop application that reads and displays the public statistics of its user’s posts (YouTube today, TikTok being set up, Instagram and Facebook planned).

2. Nature of the software

  • Pulse Studio is a personal, free and non-commercial project.
  • It is not sold or distributed to the public, and nothing is offered for download.
  • It provides no online service and creates no user accounts.
  • Its only user is its developer, for their own accounts.

3. Permitted use

  • Connect only accounts you own, or accounts whose owner has given you express permission.
  • Use the application read-only, to view your own statistics.
  • Follow the terms of the platforms involved: TikTok’s Terms of Service and Developer Terms, the YouTube API Services Terms and Google’s policies, and later Meta’s terms.

4. What the application does and does not do

  • It uses only the platforms’ official APIs, with minimal read-only permissions. It does not scrape any web page.
  • It posts nothing, changes no account, sends no messages and does not interact with any other user.
  • It respects the platforms’ request limits: the YouTube daily quota and TikTok rate limits.

5. Personal data

Data handling (statistics stored locally in SQLite, tokens in Windows Credential Manager, a Cloudflare broker for the TikTok token exchange) is described in the Privacy Policy.

6. Third-party services

Pulse Studio relies on services provided by third parties: TikTok, Google / YouTube, Cloudflare and GitHub. Their availability, their rules and the data they return are outside Pulse Studio’s control. A platform may change or withdraw access to its API at any time.

7. Accuracy of statistics

The values shown are those returned by the public APIs at the time of each reading. They may differ from the official dashboards (rounding, update delays, corrections). A temporary drop is only kept once confirmed. Statistics not provided by the APIs (retention, watch time, reach) are not shown.

10. Ending use

An account can be disconnected at any time from the application, which revokes the access granted. Access can also be removed from the platform’s account settings. Local data can be deleted at any time (see the Privacy Policy).
